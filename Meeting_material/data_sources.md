# Data Sources for Construction Safety Monitoring

## Project domain: India only

The project will focus exclusively on Indian construction sites, conditions, and safety requirements. Use Indian locations for weather queries and Indian sources for regulatory and incident context. This keeps the prototype relevant to the intended operating environment.

Do not use other countries' incident records or regulations as if they describe Indian construction sites or legal requirements. Global weather services may be used only for queries at Indian coordinates and should be described as gridded estimates rather than India-specific ground observations.

## Data needed and possible sources

| Need | Source | Data type and examples | Access and fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current and forecast weather for Indian sites | [Open-Meteo Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs) | Hourly/daily numeric weather for requested Indian coordinates: temperature, apparent temperature, relative humidity, precipitation, wind speed, and gusts. Supports heat, high-wind, and rain-related risk indicators. | Global service queried only with Indian coordinates. JSON API; no key for the free tier. Free API use is non-commercial and governed by CC BY 4.0 attribution and request limits; check current terms before deployment. Forecast and grid data are not on-site sensor readings. |
| Historical weather context for Indian sites | [Open-Meteo Historical Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api) and [NASA POWER Hourly API](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/hourly/) | Historical hourly temperature, humidity, wind, and precipitation for requested Indian coordinates. NASA POWER offers JSON, CSV, NetCDF, and ASCII, with hourly records available from 2001 to near real time. | Use only Indian locations for this project. Grid products are spatial estimates, not a substitute for a site weather station. Follow each service's attribution, license, and use terms. NASA POWER precipitation is reported in mm/hour. |
| India forecasts and warnings | [India Meteorological Department (IMD)](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/) | Official Indian forecasts, weather observations and warnings, including severe-weather bulletins. Could support local weather context and warning validation. | The site publishes public bulletins, but a stable, documented open API and bulk data terms were not confirmed for this project. Treat as a manual/official reference until access, format, and reuse terms are verified. |
| Indian government datasets | [Open Government Data Platform India](https://www.data.gov.in/) | Searchable government catalog with downloadable/API datasets; potentially useful for rainfall, labour, and other official statistics if a relevant catalog is available. | Search for a specific dataset and verify its publisher, update frequency, schema, and license before use. No suitable structured India construction-incident dataset has yet been confirmed. |
| Indian safety laws and rules | [Ministry of Labour and Employment: Acts](https://labour.gov.in/acts) and [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/) | Indian legal and regulatory documents, not sensor or incident datasets. Use to identify applicable construction-worker safety requirements and checklist items. | Confirm current in-force laws, rules, and applicability with the mentor or a qualified source before encoding requirements. |
| India-specific PPE images | No verified, clearly reusable public India-specific construction PPE image dataset identified yet. Search for datasets only when their image origin, India relevance, labels, and license can be verified. | The target data should be still images of Indian construction workers/sites with labels such as person, helmet, high-visibility vest, and (if in scope) unsafe conditions. Bounding boxes or segmentation masks are useful for object detection. | Do not assume a dataset is India-specific based on its title or platform. Record its source, license, version, geographic relevance, and annotation schema before use. If no suitable set is cleared, collect a small consented Indian-site dataset and label it. |
| India-specific construction video | No verified, openly licensed public India construction-site video dataset identified yet. The preferred project source is short, consented video recorded at an Indian site or controlled demonstration. | Video can be sampled into frames for person/PPE labels and used as sequences to evaluate tracking, alert timing, and inference speed. It may show nearby equipment or wet ground only when visible from the camera. | Obtain site/employer and participant permission; minimize face capture, protect footage, and define access and retention. Do not assume still-image dataset permissions cover video or surveillance use. |

## Important limitations and next steps

- Weather estimates cannot establish that a particular scaffold, floor, or access path at an Indian site is slippery. Site inspection or on-site sensors are needed to confirm local surface conditions.
- Public historical incident data can inform background risk in India, but does not predict a specific worker's immediate risk. Verify that any dataset is India-specific before using it as evidence.
- None of the listed public sources detects whether equipment is operating close to a worker at a specific Indian site. That requires site video/telemetry or another approved local sensor.
- Ultralytics is a model/tooling framework, not a PPE dataset. Train or evaluate a model only on India-relevant datasets whose labels and reuse terms are understood.
- Before implementation, identify an India-relevant, licensed image dataset or collect consented Indian-site images/video. Record the selected source, license, version, location context, and labels.
